What brings you to Spanish?

Everyday Spanish in Mexico City

If you’re new to Mexico City, even a quick stop for tortillas can give you something to practice. You might know how to place your order but still miss the question that comes next.

At the tortilla shop

Customer ¿Me da medio kilo, por favor?

Seller Claro. ¿Trae bolsa?

Already comfortable with everyday errands? Bring a conversation that takes more effort, like disagreeing with a coworker in a meeting. We’ll try different ways to put your point across.

DELE exam preparation, B1 to C2

If you’re preparing for the DELE (Diploma de Español como Lengua Extranjera), tell me your level and exam date. We’ll practice reading, listening, writing and speaking, spending more time on the tasks you find difficult.

For a speaking task, try giving your opinion, supporting it with an example and responding to an objection. We’ll use the exam criteria to review your answer, then you’ll try it again against the clock.

Meet your teacher

How we’ll work together

I studied Hispanic language and literature at UNAM, Mexico’s National Autonomous University. I’ve taught Spanish for more than fifteen years and hold DELE examiner accreditation from the Instituto Cervantes for C1 and C2.

In class, you tell me a story or explain an idea. I listen for the part you struggle to put into words. Together we find a phrase that fits, and you try it in your own sentence. We save those expressions in a shared document and use them again next time.

Before your first lesson

Where do we meet?

We meet online. You can start before you move to Mexico City or join from wherever you live.

Can I start as a complete beginner?

Yes. If you’ve just moved to Mexico, we might start with how to introduce yourself to a neighbor. We’ll practice both sides of that first conversation so you know what to listen for, too.

What should I bring?

Bring a situation you’d like to rehearse or a question about your Spanish. If you’re preparing for the DELE, bring your target level, your exam date if you have one, and a task you’ve found difficult.

Tell me what’s coming up.

Send me a few lines about the Spanish you want to use or the DELE level you’re preparing for. You can also choose a time for your trial lesson on Preply.
